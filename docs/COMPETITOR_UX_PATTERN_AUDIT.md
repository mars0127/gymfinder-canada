# Competitor UX Pattern Audit

**Status:** Stage 2 planning artifact — not yet complete.

## Objective

Identify proven or commonly used interaction patterns relevant to GymFinder without copying proprietary branding, assets, copy, user content, datasets, or complete screen compositions.

## Candidate products

The audit should verify current versions and relevant flows for a mixed set of direct and adjacent products, including candidates such as:

- GymMaps;
- GymBeat;
- MoreGyms;
- ClassPass;
- Wellhub;
- SweatMap;
- Toronto municipal recreation discovery tools;
- other current gym, venue, travel, property, or marketplace products whose filtering and comparison patterns are relevant.

A product name in this list is a research candidate, not an endorsement or settled source.

## Flows to inspect

1. Landing and first value.
2. Location entry and permission handling.
3. Search setup.
4. Filter hierarchy and progressive disclosure.
5. Must-have versus preferred requirements.
6. List and map relationship.
7. Result-card information hierarchy.
8. Sort and ranking explanation.
9. Comparison behavior.
10. Profile structure.
11. Equipment and amenity presentation.
12. Trust, verification, recency, and source disclosure.
13. Treatment of missing or unknown information.
14. Contribution and correction flows.
15. Account timing.
16. Empty, loading, error, and zero-result states.
17. Mobile and responsive behavior.
18. Accessibility observations.
19. Monetization patterns that should be excluded or deferred.

## Initial GymFinder hypotheses to test

These are current hypotheses, not audit conclusions:

- list-first results are better for structured comparison than map-first discovery;
- users should specify must-have and preferred requirements separately;
- result cards should show requirements met, unmet, and unknown;
- verification and freshness should be concise at first and expandable;
- gym profiles should group equipment, amenities, access, and evidence consistently;
- corrections should target one fact at a time;
- browsing should not require an account;
- a map may be secondary or deferred.

## Copyright and data boundary

Allowed:

- describing a general interaction pattern in original words;
- recreating a neutral low-fidelity pattern study;
- recording information hierarchy and state behavior;
- citing a source and retrieval date.

Not allowed:

- copying logos, artwork, photos, or marketing copy;
- reproducing a full proprietary screen composition as the GymFinder design;
- copying user reviews or restricted product content;
- importing competitor data into GymFinder;
- implying affiliation.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Direct and adjacent products are represented.
- [ ] Relevant flows are inspected rather than only marketing pages.
- [ ] Sources and dates are recorded.
- [ ] Patterns are evaluated against GymFinder's trust and data model.
- [ ] Every recommended pattern is marked adopt, adapt, reject, or defer.
- [ ] Findings are reflected in Figma.
- [ ] A concise GymFinder interaction model is recommended.
- [ ] Limitations and assumptions are explicit.
