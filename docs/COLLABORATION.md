## Decision rule

- The issue owner proposes.
- The reviewer challenges assumptions and tests acceptance criteria.
- A human founder approves.
- Important decisions are committed to `DECISIONS.md`.

## Disagreement rule

When founders or AI agents disagree:

1. State the exact decision.
2. List the alternatives.
3. Identify what evidence could distinguish them.
4. Choose the smallest reversible option when evidence is weak.
5. Record the decision and review trigger.

Do not resolve disagreements by asking multiple AI chats until one gives the preferred answer.

## Communication hygiene

- Put decisions in GitHub, not only in messages.
- Link research notes to the issue they inform.
- Keep private participant details outside the repository.
- Use pull-request comments for line-specific feedback.
- Close an issue only when its acceptance criteria are verified.
