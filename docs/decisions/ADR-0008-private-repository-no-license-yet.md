# ADR-0008: Private Repository With No Public Licence Yet

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

The founders have not decided whether the project will be proprietary, open source, or partially open. Adding a public licence prematurely grants rights that may not match the eventual business strategy.

## Decision

Keep the repository private during discovery and initial implementation. Do not add an open-source licence until both founders make an explicit decision.

## Consequences

- Collaboration occurs through invited access.
- External contributions are not accepted yet.
- Reference documents and early research remain private.
- A future licensing decision must consider code, data, and third-party source obligations separately.
