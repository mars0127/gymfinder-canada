# ADR-0001: Launch Toronto-First

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

A Canada-wide seed database would create broad nominal coverage but mostly incomplete profiles. The product's value depends on equipment and facility facts layered onto a location, not the presence of a map pin.

## Decision

The public MVP will focus on the City of Toronto. Initial research may concentrate on North York and selected additional Toronto areas where the founders can obtain and verify data.

## Alternatives considered

- Ontario-wide launch.
- Canada-wide launch.
- GTA-wide launch.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Higher profile completeness.
- Lower maintenance burden.
- Easier founder verification.
- Clearer local positioning.

### Negative

- Smaller initial audience.
- The working name may imply broader coverage than the MVP provides.

## Review trigger

Review expansion only after Toronto freshness and correction workflows are sustainable and the MVP demonstrates useful matching.
