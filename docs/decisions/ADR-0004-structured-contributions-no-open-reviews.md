# ADR-0004: Structured Contributions; No Open Reviews in MVP

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

The core product needs corrections to equipment and facility facts. Open-ended reviews add moderation, harassment, manipulation, and defamation risk while duplicating existing review platforms.

## Decision

The MVP will accept structured confirmations, denials, and proposed facts. It will not host general free-text gym reviews or star ratings.

## Consequences

- Lower moderation surface.
- Better structured data.
- Less room for subjective atmosphere information in the first version.
- Optional factual notes must remain tightly scoped and moderated if introduced later.
