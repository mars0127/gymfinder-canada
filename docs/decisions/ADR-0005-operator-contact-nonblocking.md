# ADR-0005: Gym-Operator Participation Is Non-Blocking

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Context

The founders may not currently be able to contact gym operators consistently. Requiring operator responses would delay or prevent planning, data collection, and launch.

## Decision

The MVP and project plan must function without gym partnerships, operator interviews, a claimed-profile dashboard, or trial-code commitments.

Operator outreach may be attempted later when a credible sample profile or beta creates a concrete reason to respond.

## Consequences

- Seed data depends on permitted public sources, founder research, and community correction.
- Staff confirmation remains a future verification source, not a launch requirement.
- Trial rewards and business analytics are deferred.
- A lightweight claim-interest mechanism may be added later without redesigning the core data model.

## Review trigger

Review after a useful public prototype exists or when a critical field proves unobtainable without direct business confirmation.
